## Gaurav Parthasarathy

Hi, I am a software engineer at Veezoo. I enjoy developing *trustworthy* software.

I recently completed my PhD in computer science at ETH Zurich under the supervision of [Peter Müller](https://www.pm.inf.ethz.ch/people/person-detail.html?persid=112017) (main advisor) and [Alex Summers](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~alexsumm/).
In my PhD, I developed techniques for formally verifying the correctness of automatic program verifiers, which are tools that automatically check whether programs do not have any bugs.
In short: I verified verifiers.
A distinguishing aspect of my research is that it targets *existing* automatic program verifiers *used in practice*.
